  1499→# 7. The System-2 → System-1 idea actually became code
  1500→
  1501→This was one of the best hidden recoveries.
  1502→
  1503→GitHub contains multiple reflex-cache and reflex-compiler implementations.
  1504→
  1505→The Rust `ReflexCompiler` explicitly describes itself as:
  1506→
  1507→> `System-2 traces -> System-1 rules`
  1508→
  1509→and uses repeated samples, Ihsān, SNR, low path variance, policy hashes, and quarantine metadata before producing a reflex rule.
  1510→
  1511→So this older sequence:
  1512→
  1513→```text
  1514→slow reasoning
  1515→     ↓
  1516→successful repetition
  1517→     ↓
  1518→pattern detection
  1519→     ↓
  1520→reflex compilation
  1521→     ↓
  1522→O(1)-like reuse
  1523→```
  1524→
  1525→was not merely conceptual.
  1526→
  1527→### But today's architecture exposes a missing upgrade
  1528→
  1529→The older compiler essentially records “successful” samples plus a `guardian_approved` flag.
  1530→
  1531→The mature law now needs to be:
  1532→
  1533→```text
  1534→execution
  1535→   ↓
  1536→independent observation
  1537→   ↓
  1538→verified experience
  1539→   ↓
  1540→repeated verified convergence
  1541→   ↓
  1542→reflex candidate
  1543→   ↓
  1544→policy/contract binding
  1545→   ↓
  1546→compiled reflex
  1547→```
  1548→
  1549→Not:
  1550→
  1551→```text
  1552→agent believes success
  1553→   ↓
  1554→compile
  1555→```
  1556→
  1557→So the reflex engine should eventually consume **verified RCST/receipt-bound experience**, not merely successful traces.
  1558→
  1559→That is a very high-SNR bridge between old BIZRA and current BIZRA.

  1568→

  1570→
  1571→```text
  1572→vector      0.40
  1573→keyword     0.15
  1574→graph-hop   0.25
  1575→recency     0.10
  1576→importance  0.10
  1577→```
  1578→
  1579→But there are two concrete implementation observations hidden beneath the impressive architecture.
  1580→

  1586→

  1599→# 9. ThoughtPacket was a very strong idea and should not disappear
  1600→
  1601→The old Agentic Realm architecture did **not** propose exposing raw hidden reasoning between agents.
  1602→
  1603→It proposed structured packets such as:
  1604→
  1605→```text
  1606→hypothesis
  1607→evidence
  1608→objection
  1609→proposal
  1610→risk
  1611→verification
  1612→counterexample

  1615→```
  1616→
  1617→That is significantly better architecture than agents passing giant opaque natural-language transcripts to one another.
  1618→
  1619→And it has partly survived.
  1620→
  1621→Dema's current Covenant Gate prototype explicitly uses a `ThoughtPacket` concept combined with decision-bound micro-consent and receipts. It also names `minority_report` and `consensus` as next taxonomy extensions.
  1622→
  1623→### The hidden gem
  1624→
  1625→A **minority report** is not merely an alternate answer.
  1626→
  1627→It is a constitutional circuit breaker:
  1628→
  1629→```text
  1630→majority hypothesis
  1631→        │
  1632→        ├──────────────┐
  1633→        ▼              ▼
  1634→     consensus    minority report
  1635→        │              │
  1636→        └──── proof ────┘
  1637→```
  1638→
  1639→One verified counterexample may outweigh six agreeing agents.
  1640→
  1641→This is the correct implementation of:
  1642→
  1643→> disagreement as epistemic fuel.

  1647→# 10. The master failure pattern across the entire history is **proof-substitution drift**
  1648→
  1649→This appears again and again under different names.
  1650→
  1651→### Pattern
  1652→
  1653→```text
  1654→a valid weak proof
  1655→      ↓
  1656→works well
  1657→      ↓
  1658→gets familiar
  1659→      ↓
  1660→quietly promoted
  1661→      ↓
  1662→used as proof of a stronger claim
  1663→```
  1664→
  1665→Examples across the history:
  1666→
  1667→| Weak proofIncorrect promotionStronger law eventually learned |                                       |                                                              |
  1668→| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
  1669→| Hash/signature chain                                         | External world truly behaved that way | Integrity ≠ empirical truth                                  |
  1670→| Agent consensus                                              | Independent verification              | Different processes can share failure modes                  |
  1671→| Numeric Ihsān aggregate                                      | Constitutionally permitted            | Hard violations cannot be averaged away                      |
  1672→| Test success                                                 | Production behavior                   | Synthetic/test evidence cannot settle production             |
  1673→| Valid consent                                                | Correct outcome                       | Authority ≠ truth                                            |
  1674→| Execution exit `0`                                           | Effect committed                      | Execution ≠ independently observed postcondition             |
  1675→| Mutable sprint DAG                                           | Parent production contract            | Child state cannot rewrite frozen acceptance law             |
  1676→| 64-hex replay hash                                           | Corroboration                         | Replay must bind the exact evidence/hypothesis/claim subject |
  1677→| Repeated document                                            | Independent corroboration             | Duplicate copies are still one lineage                       |
  1678→
  1679→This is the single most important lesson recovered.
  1680→
  1681→The present Dema capability registry is already moving in the right direction: each capability row carries `what_this_proves`, `what_this_does_not_prove`, promotion rules, dependencies, blocked conditions, and explicit forbidden live claims.
  1682→
  1683→### Golden law
  1684→
  1685→> **Every proof artifact needs an explicit proof ceiling.**
  1686→
  1687→Not only:
  1688→
  1689→```text
  1690→evidence = X
  1691→```
  1692→
  1693→but:
  1694→
  1695→```text
  1696→X proves:
  1697→  A
  1698→  B
  1699→
  1700→X explicitly does NOT prove:
  1701→  C
  1702→  D
  1703→  E
  1704→```
  1705→
  1706→That should become universal.
